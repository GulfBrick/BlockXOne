# Phase 4 Plan 01: Verify KYC And Wallet State As One Compliance Workflow

## Objective

- **What:** Fix and verify KYC submission, queue, approval, rejection, wallet approval, and downstream eligibility behavior.
- **Why:** `blockxone` cannot claim compliant investor gating if the route handlers, policy checks, and persisted state disagree about who is eligible.
- **Output:** Evidence-backed compliance transitions with queue visibility, correct eligibility, and auditable wallet state propagation.
