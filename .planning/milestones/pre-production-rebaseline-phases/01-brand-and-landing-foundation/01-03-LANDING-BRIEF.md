# Phase 1 Deliverable: Landing Page Brief

## Page Objective

Explain BlockXOne clearly enough that:
- institutions understand the operating model
- investors understand the path to access
- the product does not feel like a generic crypto app

## Positioning

### Primary positioning statement

BlockXOne is a multi-asset tokenization platform for regulated private-market assets, combining investor onboarding, compliance controls, issuance, servicing, and reporting in one operating system.

### Tone

- institutional
- precise
- modern
- calm
- trustworthy

Avoid:
- hype language
- vague "revolutionizing finance" copy
- unverified compliance claims
- wallet-first jargon on the public surface

## Hero

### Headline

**Tokenize and operate private-market assets with institutional control.**

### Subhead

Launch, govern, and service tokenized funds, private credit, real-estate vehicles, and structured products with built-in onboarding, compliance, issuance, and reporting.

### Primary CTAs

- `Request demo`
- `Investor login`
- `Operator login`

### Supporting proof line

Built for investor onboarding, eligibility controls, token lifecycle operations, and auditable servicing.

## Section Copy Direction

### 1. What tokenization means

Message:
- tokenization is not just minting a token
- it is structuring, onboarding, eligibility, issuance, servicing, and reporting around a digital asset

### 2. Supported asset classes

Message:
- BlockXOne is multi-asset by architecture
- launch focus is regulated funds and adjacent private-market instruments

Cards:
- Private funds
- Private credit
- Real-estate vehicles
- Structured debt and cash-equivalent products

### 3. How BlockXOne works

Use a six-step lifecycle:
- Structure the asset
- Onboard and qualify investors
- Approve subscriptions and settlement
- Issue controlled on-chain positions
- Service distributions, reporting, and lifecycle events
- Redeem or transfer under policy controls

### 4. Built for institutions

Message:
- operator-grade controls
- compliance review
- role-based administration
- audit history
- workflow visibility

### 5. Investor experience

Message:
- transparent onboarding
- qualification status
- asset discovery
- portfolio and documents
- redemption workflow

### 6. Operator experience

Message:
- issuance setup
- compliance queues
- treasury and servicing
- token controls
- reporting and audit

### 7. Security and compliance posture

Allowed claims:
- role-based access
- auditability
- eligibility and transfer controls
- workflow traceability

Blocked claims until verified:
- SOC 2 compliant
- ISO 27001 certified
- GDPR compliant
- WCAG conformance badges

Public copy can describe the intended control posture, but should not state certifications that are not proven.

## Visual Direction

- Replace the current video-first hero with content-first composition
- Use diagrams, structured cards, and lifecycle framing instead of generic motion-first panels
- Keep glow effects subtle and secondary to typography and layout
